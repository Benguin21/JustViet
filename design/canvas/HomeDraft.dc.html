  1. Open this board’s Tweaks. In Goals settings, switch Vocab SRS, Grammar and Skills on or off.
  2. In Demo data, raise words studied, grammar done and skills done today until a card says “Ready to feed”.
  3. Tap a ready card’s check to feed Rồng Con. A fed goal stays checked for the rest of the day.
  4. Feed one goal, then switch another goal off. The eaten dish stays put.
  5. Change Restart demo to start the day over.
  6. Change Replay intro to see Cô Tươi and Thầy Vinh’s first-visit intro again.
  7. To watch the egg hatch at Level 5 (700 XP), set XP before today to 690, then feed a goal. It plays once; Restart demo lets you see it again.
  8. Each level reached for the first time gets a little “Level up!” cue. Rồng Con grows its full body at Level 10 (1,960 XP).
